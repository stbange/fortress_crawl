# Change: add-realms-behind-the-gate

## Why
The fortress was endless but alike: every place was a dungeon. Worlds of their own behind a gate give wonder, new rules and rulers to beat, and tie into the history.

## What Changes
- Twenty-one realms, 6 to 8 joined to each world by its seed, each 256 m across with its own terrain, sky, flora, fauna, danger and ruler.
- Rifts in the fortress and the Gate in the Sanctum's court lead there; an arch leads back.
- Each realm's rules: weight, air, sun, storms, tides, spores, rot, mana drain, growth.
- Two sealed realms open at the end of the story.
- Save version 25: attuned, beaten, seen; plants and animals met; where you went through from.

## Impact
- Affected specs: realms
- Affected code and docs: src/world/realms.c, gen_realm.c, src/game/gate.c; docs/REALMS.md; save v25.
