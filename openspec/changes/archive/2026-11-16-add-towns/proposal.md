# Change: add-towns

## Why
The fortress had no one in it who was not an enemy. Towns of friendly peoples give rest, trade, company and people to help, and make the world feel lived in.

## What Changes
- Seven peoples (dwarves, goblins, gnomes, elves, humans, halflings, orcs) in towns, one always about 200 m east of the gate.
- Innkeeper, healer, merchants and smith, elder with tasks, guards, families and children.
- Reputation from stranger to kin, saved with the world (save version 26).

## Impact
- Affected specs: towns
- Affected code and docs: src/world/towns.c, gen_town.c, src/game/town.c; docs/TOWNS.md; save v26.
