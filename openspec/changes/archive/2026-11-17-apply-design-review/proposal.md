# Change: apply-design-review

## Why
A review of the design found the climax skippable (two strikes ended the game), two competing main stories, a game too safe with gold piling up, towns outside the story, loot that was all weapons, no endgame, and co-op choices taken by whoever chose first.

## What Changes
- The king listens only when brought to his knees; each ending has its own reward.
- One main story: the chronicle's end completes step 8; one line at the top (Y switches); only the king opens the Astral Shore.
- The way teaches: a hint the first time the line follows a step.
- Towns: trust opens wares instead of discounts, the town's works for gold, kin in the Sanctum, raids, celebration.
- His servants close in; dragon-scale armour; heirs; co-op votes; back where you fell once in five minutes.
- Save version 29.
