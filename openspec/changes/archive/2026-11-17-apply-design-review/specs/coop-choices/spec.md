## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Choosing at those who yield
In co-op, every player at one who yields SHALL choose; the most SHALL decide, a tie spares, and after 25 s those who chose decide. Alone, the choice SHALL be made at once.

#### Scenario: Two against one
- **WHEN** two players choose spare and one chooses kill
- **THEN** the target is spared and a banner says what was chosen together

### Requirement: Choosing at the king
In co-op, more than half of the players within 40 m of the faltering king SHALL strike for the same ending before it is settled; the sword needs no vote.

#### Scenario: Not yet
- **WHEN** one of two players speaks his name
- **THEN** a banner says 1 of 2 want it and the other must strike too
