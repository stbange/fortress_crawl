# Change: add-dragons

## Why
The game needed huge, terrifying enemies with tons of loot, and the Old King's way needed dragons to slay, rob and fight beside his throne.

## What Changes
- Twenty-five kinds in five sizes: dragonets in swarms, drakes, wyverns, great dragons and ancient wyrms.
- Lairs with hoards; great dragons asleep on them; a fight in three phases; breath through the world simulation.
- The richest loot in the game; slain dragons stay dead (save version 27).

## Impact
- Affected specs: dragons
- Affected code and docs: src/world/dragons.c, gen_lair.c, src/game/dragon.c; docs/DRAGONS.md; save v27; new sounds.
