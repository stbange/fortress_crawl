## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A history per world
When a world begins, its past SHALL be simulated from the seed: founders, wars, bargains, heresies, keepers, heirs and rivals, the night the seal was opened and the lie told about it; who opened it and why, whom they blamed and what lies below SHALL differ between worlds.

#### Scenario: Two seeds
- **WHEN** two worlds are compared
- **THEN** their openers, motives and what lies below usually differ

### Requirement: Motives
Every character SHALL choose a motive when they first enter a world (revenge, glory, riches or truth), which SHALL turn the world's metaplot into their chapters.

#### Scenario: Choosing
- **WHEN** a new character enters a world
- **THEN** they are asked why they came

### Requirement: Chapters
Chapter targets SHALL be named survivors of the history, each a boss farther from the gate than the last: a captain, then the general above, then the Power they serve; every kill SHALL reveal more and point to the next.

#### Scenario: A chapter
- **WHEN** a player kills their chapter target
- **THEN** its words and papers are read and the next target is named

### Requirement: Traces
Inscriptions, notes of dead adventurers (some asking that something be taken home) and bounty boards SHALL lie in the dungeon; standing next to one SHALL show its text, and everything read SHALL go into the STORY tab.

#### Scenario: A note
- **WHEN** a player stands at a dead adventurer's note
- **THEN** its text shows and the STORY tab keeps it

### Requirement: Domains
Each region SHALL be a domain with a name, a ruling faction, a past and a lord; entering one SHALL show a banner.

#### Scenario: Entering
- **WHEN** a player crosses into another domain
- **THEN** a banner names it and its lord

### Requirement: Open questions
The STORY tab SHALL keep the world's open questions (who opened the seal, why the village burned, ...) and answer each once enough clues are found; some clues SHALL lie.

#### Scenario: Answered
- **WHEN** a player has found enough clues to who opened the seal
- **THEN** the STORY tab answers it

### Requirement: Hunters
Ten minutes without a chapter finished, the current target SHALL send hunters (an elite and two of its kind) from out of sight, and the Golden Thread SHALL show the way back to whoever sent them.

#### Scenario: Too slow
- **WHEN** a player finishes no chapter for ten minutes
- **THEN** hunters come for them
