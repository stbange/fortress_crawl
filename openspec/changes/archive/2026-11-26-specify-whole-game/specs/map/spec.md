## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The map
M SHALL show the map of the cells the player has seen on the layer they are on, named (gate level, layer 3 below, ...), with the staircases seen and the story target marked in red; the explored map near the player SHALL be saved.

#### Scenario: Opening
- **WHEN** a player presses M
- **THEN** the explored cells of their layer show, with stairs marked
