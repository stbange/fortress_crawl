## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Smithing
Smithing SHALL be a skill from 1 to 10 learned only by working at the forge, a Sanctum plot from the island's second stage.

#### Scenario: Practice
- **WHEN** a player forges pieces
- **THEN** their smithing rises

### Requirement: Materials
Ore SHALL be mined from veins (silver north, mithril in the deep, adamant in the hells, star ore far out) and smelted with charcoal and flux; essences SHALL come from monsters of a region's element and titan hearts from bosses; the smith SHALL sell charcoal, flux, quenching oil, rune ink, steel and moonsilver.

#### Scenario: Mithril
- **WHEN** a player mines a vein in the deep
- **THEN** they get mithril ore

### Requirement: Blueprints and runes
Breaking weapons up SHALL teach their blueprints and the runes of their powers.

#### Scenario: A blueprint
- **WHEN** a player breaks up a flail
- **THEN** they learn its blueprint

### Requirement: Forging
In the FORGE tab the player SHALL choose blueprint, metal, essences (affixes), runes (powers), a titan heart and oil, then work the metal: the bellows heat it, it cools, and each strike SHALL count by how close it is to the metal's heat window; the quality, metal and skill SHALL set the level, rarity and material.

#### Scenario: A perfect strike
- **WHEN** a player strikes while the heat is in the window
- **THEN** the piece's quality rises most

### Requirement: Reforging
Reforging SHALL bring a weapon up to the player's level and beyond.

#### Scenario: An old favourite
- **WHEN** a player reforges a level-5 sword at level 20
- **THEN** it comes out at about level 20 or more
