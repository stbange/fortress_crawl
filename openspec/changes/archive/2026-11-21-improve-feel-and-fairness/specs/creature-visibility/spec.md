## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Creatures stand out of the dark
Creatures SHALL be lit at no less than 0.3 of full light and SHALL carry a rim in their glow colour on faces seen edge-on, so that their silhouettes reach at least 3.5:1 luminance contrast against the stone round them; the rooms' own lighting is unchanged.

#### Scenario: A dragon in its cave
- **WHEN** the dragon fight demo is captured
- **THEN** the dragon's body measures 3.5:1 or better against the rock behind it
