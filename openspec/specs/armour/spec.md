# armour Specification

## Purpose
Dragon-scale armour, the one piece of armour, made at the Sanctum's anvil.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Dragon-scale armour
The anvil SHALL make dragon-scale armour in three makes, each replacing the one before: drake-scale mail (smithing 2, 6 essences of one element, 2 dragon's teeth, 3 steel; 10% less damage), dragon-scale mail (smithing 5, 10, 4, 2 mithril; 17%), wyrm-scale plate (smithing 8, 14, 6, 2 adamant and a titan heart; 25%). Its element SHALL be the essence held most, and that element's after-effects SHALL not take hold. It SHALL show in the inventory and be saved with the character.

#### Scenario: Fire mail
- **WHEN** a player in fire drake-scale mail is struck by fire
- **THEN** they take 10% less and do not burn
