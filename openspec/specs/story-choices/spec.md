# story-choices Specification

## Purpose
What a player decides at those who yield and at the chronicle's finale, and what comes of it.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Yielding
A chapter target near death SHALL yield, and the player SHALL choose: kill it, spare it (it tells more and returns grateful or with a grudge) or take its offer (its people let the player pass, their enemies hunt them).

#### Scenario: Spared
- **WHEN** a player spares a yielding captain
- **THEN** it tells what it knows and leaves

### Requirement: The finale
The chronicle's finale SHALL end with the one who opened the seal killed, allowed to finish, or the key taken, and the arcs after it SHALL follow from what the player did.

#### Scenario: The key
- **WHEN** a player takes the key
- **THEN** the arc after the finale follows from that choice

### Requirement: Consequences
What a player decided SHALL change the world: killed lords leave their domains empty or to rivals, posters on the boards name the player, the grateful live in the Sanctum, and the STORY tab SHALL list the deeds and what came of them.

#### Scenario: A poster
- **WHEN** a player has killed many who yielded
- **THEN** bounty boards name them by a title such as the Butcher
