# Ten runs, told as stories

November 2026. Ten playtest-bot runs of 90 minutes each, in two worlds (seed 31337, whose
fortress is Cedmark, and seed 8080, whose fortress is Aldburg), five play styles each. Taken
from the bots' logs, which now record every banner and story message.

## Cedmark (seed 31337)

### The archer: the Book of Halawen
Truth was what it wanted: Ostoric the Last kept the first page of the Book of Halawen beneath
Cedmark. It found him in two minutes and won the page, two spells and an epic shortbow at once;
the page ended mid-sentence, pointing to Uloos Bone-Crowned. Uloos was never found, but sent
hunters after it five times. The archer went home at minute 17 and furnished the Sanctum with a
table, a cold pool and a fountain, then pushed outward through sealed vaults into the second
step out. There the loot turned golden: four legendaries in two minutes, then two of the
world's own from named enemies, Widowveil and Ruinword (and later Widowveil a second time). At
minute 69 it broke up a crossbow at its new forge and forged a silver one; it bought a statue of
the founder, opened a vault's hoard, killed Ysaaine the Smiling for Oathwarden, and ended at the
anvil with a 96% silver crossbow. Level 18, 931 kills, no deaths, still chapter 2.

### The caster: the fire at Cedbrook
Revenge: its father died in the chapel at Cedbrook, and Ostoric the Last held the bar on the
door. Ostoric sent hunters first; at minute 17 the caster found him, dead with Uloos
Bone-Crowned's coin in his mouth: Uloos had paid for the fire. From then on Uloos's hunters came
every ten minutes, and the caster never found Uloos. It went home late (minute 45), learned the
Star Elixir and two torn halves, reached the second step out, and at minute 60 killed Nefemun
the Hollow for Oaththorn, which it carried to the end. Level 14, 432 kills, no deaths, three
trips home.

### The delver: the map to Cedawen's Treasury
It wanted gold and went farther than anyone: the third step out, more than a kilometre from the
gate. Two named enemies fell in its first four minutes; Velimir of the Black Lantern gave it
Starthorn and Draeka Saltmouth Widowveil, which it carried all game and which, with 302 kills
on it, earned the title Slimebane. It died three times (burning in the first step, a horned
beast, an elite knight just after killing Shenomir the Unburied for Oathwarden) and walked
through long stretches of plague-ground vaults. At minute 64 it built the forge, broke up five
weapons for their blueprints and runes, and forged an axe; by the end it had a blast furnace,
smithing 4 and a 100% silver greatsword of storms at level 30. Ostoric the Last, the first name
of its story, sent hunters seven times and was never sought out.

### The explorer: the Roll of the Dead
Glory: kill Ostoric the Last and have its name carved on the Roll at the gate. It killed Haleos
Ash-Tongue, then Nefahep Gravewed for Ravensong and Ashumun the Smiling for Hollowfang, built the
first comforts at home, and took two missions. After minute 42 its story stops: the bot got stuck
again and again (94 times) wandering the first step out. Level 12, no deaths.

### The quester: the Roll of the Dead, the whole way
The only run that told its story. It died in the first minute to a boss brute by the vestibule,
came back, and within a minute had its name carved on the Roll and the next one above it: Uloos
Bone-Crowned. Uloos sent hunters at minute 22; at 28 the quester killed him and found a ledger:
"The gate of Cedmark was not broken. It was opened from inside." Only Gerewen stood above it now.
Two more deaths to boss brutes; then at minute 38 Gerewen fell, saying, dying, "I only opened the
door. Ask Velimir who walked through." The quester's name stood first on the Roll, until
tomorrow: a new name was carved, Shenimir Saltmouth. It carried away Griefbrand and three relics.
Then the bot got stuck high up for most of the last 50 minutes. Chapter 4, level 14, four deaths.

## Aldburg (seed 8080)

### The archer: the Drowned Gold of Vareos
Treasure: Varard the Thrice-Hanged kept the first piece of the map. The archer never found him;
he sent hunters six times. It collected missions instead (bounties, regimental colours,
wooden horses, a brother's ashes), went home four times to build the table, bath, fountain and
finally a kitchen, and from minute 42 killed three named enemies, each for one of the world's
legendaries: Wyrmbrand, Dawnheart and Wyrmthorn, which it kept. Level 14, 547 kills, no deaths.

### The caster: Aldimund's Ledger
Truth, kept by Varard. It never reached him, but killed five named enemies for Wyrmbrand,
Starreaver, Ironfang and Stormsong, and learned Storm in a Bottle on the way. At minute 56 it
spent its savings on a wider island, a forge, a library, an armory and a garden in one trip, and
became a smith: four burning scepters (100%, 95%, 90%, 100%), smithing 4, a blast furnace by
minute 76. Level 17, 874 kills, no deaths.

### The delver: the Roll of the Dead, far out
It went the farthest of all ten: the fourth step out, 1.1 km from the gate, and level 18.
Ulfifrid the Grey gave it Dawnsong at minute 5, a vault's hoard gave it Time Syrup and half the
Phoenix Elixir, and Velaana Lantern-Jaw gave it Starreaver. It died twice in the third step out,
to fire and to a knight, then completed the Phoenix Elixir from its second half. In its last
minute at home it built the wider island, a forge, a library, an armory, a garden and the
founder's statue. Its weapon at the end: a tempered crystal dagger at level 39. Varard, first on
its Roll, sent hunters seven times.

### The explorer: the fire at Cedstead
Revenge for a son burned in the chapel at Cedstead. This one became a smith before anyone else:
at minute 13 it built the wider island and the forge and forged a burning sword, and kept at it
(swords, then silver greatswords, six in all, smithing 5 by minute 78). It killed seven named
enemies (Ulwyn the Thrice-Hanged, Varewen the Hollow, Ostmund the Unwashed, Ostios the Flayed,
Ediwyn Nine-Fingers, Vyyulf Lantern-Jaw, Malioc Saltmouth), learned a dozen formulae including
the Phoenix Elixir, and built the Sanctum up most of all: hall, library, armory with the banners
of its foes, garden and a marble bath. It fought to the end with a sword it forged itself,
named Slimebane after 243 kills. Varard, who held the bar, was never found. No deaths.

### The quester: the fire at Cedstead, quickly avenged
The same revenge, and it found Varard in two minutes (after dying once to a boss brute): dead
with Ulimund the Unburied's coin in his mouth. Ulimund paid for the fire. Ulimund sent hunters
every ten minutes; the quester killed Cedawyn, Mother of Worms for Ruinveil and went home once.
After minute 43 the bot was stuck somewhere high (170 m up) and the story stopped. Level 10.

## What the ten runs show

- **The story moves only for those who chase it.** Only the questers advanced it (one reached
  chapter 4 with a good mystery: the gate opened from inside, Gerewen, Velimir, Shenimir). The
  others met their first target's hunters every ten minutes, all game long, but not the target.
  The hunters are reliable; perhaps they should carry the next clue, or a beaten patrol should
  reveal the target's place.
- **Named enemies and the world's legendaries are everywhere.** Every run killed several named
  enemies and carried a legendary of its world. Twice the same world legendary dropped twice
  (Widowveil, Griefknell): a bug to fix, since each should be found once.
- **Random legendaries are too common.** 2 to 18 per run, most from the second step out; they
  devalue both the named legendaries and the forge's masterworks.
- **Deaths are rare and mostly the same.** 10 in 15 hours: half to elite or boss brutes in the
  first steps, the rest in the third step out.
- **The forge found its players.** Five of ten runs built it and four forged; one explorer made
  it the centre of its game from minute 13 to smithing 5.
- **The bot still gets stuck** (three runs lost their second half), which says more about the
  bot than the game.
